CLARIFICATIONS & POLICIES

Frequently Asked Questions.

Clear, factual answers regarding delivery times, formulation, prepaid payments, and return eligibility.

Why are orders shipped via surface courier instead of air cargo? +
Ethanol-based fine perfumes are classified as flammable liquids under international civil aviation (IATA) safety regulations and are legally prohibited from commercial passenger air transit. To ensure 100% legal compliance and safe transit, all parcels travel via dedicated ground surface logistics through Shiprocket partners.
What is the perfume for men price structure and delivery timeline? +
Our standard catalog pricing starts at ₹499 (MRP ₹700) for 50ml Eau de Parfum bottles. Surface transit across serviceable Indian pin codes takes between 3 to 9 business days (Metro cities arrive within 3 to 5 business days with SMS/email AWB tracking).
How does an Eau de Parfum compare to a body perfume or deodorant? +
Unlike a conventional aerosol body spray or alcohol-diluted deodorant that dissipates within an hour, our Eau de Parfum (EDP) formulations carry concentrated fine perfume oils designed for enduring skin intimacy and projection in Indian humidity.
Why do we operate strictly on prepaid orders (no COD)? +
To maintain our direct-to-consumer price point of ₹499 (MRP ₹700) and absorb high courier return fees caused by arbitrary COD rejections, we require 100% prepaid orders. All payments are processed through Cashfree Payments, an RBI-authorised payment aggregator.
What is the exact return policy and timeline? +
You have 7 days from the delivery date to initiate a return. For hygiene and safety standards, returned items must be unopened, in their original packaging, with all seals and cellophane intact. If an item arrives broken or defective, we pay return shipping.
What bottle size and fragrance concentration do you offer? +
Our standard releases are bottled in 50 ml Eau de Parfum (EDP) format. Eau de Parfum features a higher concentration of fine perfume oils compared to Eau de Toilette or body deodorants, engineered to linger gracefully throughout the day.
